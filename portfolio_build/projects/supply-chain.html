Supply Chain & Operations Analytics

Examined supply chain data to understand how business and operational performance varied across the order lifecycle, from fulfillment and delivery to profitability.

Business Objective

The work focused on connecting different aspects of business and operational performance to understand where inefficiencies and bottlenecks were occurring. Looking across fulfillment, delivery, shipping, and profitability helped highlight important performance patterns and areas that could support better operational decisions.

Key Findings

Order volume increased over time, but sales did not increase proportionally.
Sales and profit did not consistently move together.
Premium shipping modes did not guarantee better delivery performance.
Shipping mode was significantly associated with delivery delays.
First Class recorded a 100% late-delivery rate in the analyzed data.
Higher discount rates were associated with lower profitability.

Business Recommendations

Focus on profitable growth by monitoring order volume alongside sales growth and AOV.
Evaluate shipping modes based on actual delivery performance.
Review shipping commitments against actual delivery performance.
Optimize discounting to avoid unnecessary profitability reduction.
