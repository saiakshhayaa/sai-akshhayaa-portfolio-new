06 / CASE STUDY

Bank Loan Analysis

Assessed loan data to understand portfolio performance and loan quality across different loan and borrower dimensions, providing a clearer view of lending outcomes.

GitHub Repository ↗

Business Objective

The work provided a clearer picture of loan portfolio performance and how it varies across different loan and borrower characteristics. Bringing together application, funding, repayment, and loan-quality information helped highlight portfolio patterns and areas that may require closer attention.

Dataset

RECORDS38,575
COLUMNS24
TOOLSSQL · Power BI · DAX
FOCUSLoan portfolio performance

Analytical Approach

SQL Analysis

SQL was used for KPI calculations, aggregations, and validation.

Power BI

The report presents portfolio performance, loan quality, borrower dimensions, and record-level details.

Power BI Report

01 — Overview

Provides a consolidated view of loan portfolio performance, including key KPIs, MTD and MoM trends, Good vs. Bad Loan analysis, loan status, and key loan dimensions.

02 — Summary

Uses a DAX-based measure selector to switch between Loan Applications, Funded Amount, and Amount Received across multiple loan and borrower dimensions such as month, state, term, employment length, purpose, and home ownership.

The selected metric updates across the visuals, allowing different aspects of loan and borrower performance to be explored interactively.

03 — Details

Provides a record-level view of individual loans, including key loan and borrower attributes for detailed analysis.

Key Insights

Loan quality: Good loans formed the majority of the portfolio, while bad loans remained an important segment to monitor.
Loan purpose: Debt consolidation was the leading loan purpose.
Loan term: 36-month loans accounted for a larger share than 60-month loans.
Employment history: Borrowers with longer employment histories contributed a higher lending amount.
