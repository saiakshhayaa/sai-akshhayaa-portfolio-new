04 / CASE STUDY

Excel Sales Dashboard

Built an interactive view of sales data to understand changes in business performance and profitability across time, products, customers, and different business scenarios.

GitHub Repository ↗

Business Objective

The dashboard brings detailed sales data into a clear and interactive reporting view, making it easier to understand overall performance and identify differences across products, customers, regions, and time periods. It also supports what-if scenario exploration for more informed business decisions.

Dataset

DATASETMicrosoft Adventure Works Sales Dataset
FACT TABLE60,397 records
COLUMNS26
PLATFORMMicrosoft Excel

Dashboard Structure

01 — Time Analysis

Helps identify how business performance changes over time and where profit is concentrated across different periods.

02 — Product & Customer Analysis

Helps understand where profitability is concentrated across the product and customer base, including differences across customer characteristics and markets.

03 — Scenario Analysis

Allows users to compare different transaction-growth assumptions and evaluate their corresponding impact on revenue and profit.

Key Insights

Business Performance: 2007–2008 generated 66.5% of total revenue, 67.1% of total profit, and 93.9% of total transactions.
Product Profitability: The top five profitable products contributed 24.8% of total profit.
Customer Profitability: The top five customers contributed only 0.3% of total profit.
Customer Demographics: Customers aged 50+ contributed 42.5% of total profit.
