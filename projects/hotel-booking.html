02 / CASE STUDY

Hotel Booking Analysis

Explored hotel booking data to uncover patterns in booking behavior, identify factors associated with cancellations, and understand changes in demand and pricing.

GitHub Repository ↗

Business Questions / Objective

A key focus was understanding what influences hotel booking outcomes and cancellations, particularly how customer behavior, booking timing, seasonal demand, length of stay, and pricing patterns are connected. These patterns helped provide useful insights into booking behavior and hotel performance.

Exploratory & Statistical Analysis

The analysis examined booking behavior, customer patterns, cancellation trends, ADR and pricing patterns, and operational performance.

Welch's t-testChi-square Test of Independence

Key Findings

City hotels attracted more bookings than resort hotels.
Approximately one-third of bookings were cancelled.
First-time guests showed higher cancellation rates than repeat guests.
Bookings made well in advance showed a higher tendency to be cancelled.
ADR followed a seasonal pattern, reaching its highest in August.
Most bookings were concentrated around shorter stays.
No strong relationship was observed between length of stay and ADR.
Bookings through Online Travel Agencies showed higher cancellation rates.

Business Recommendations

Strengthen customer loyalty programs and personalized incentives to encourage repeat bookings.
Introduce booking reminders and reconfirmation emails for reservations made well in advance.
Adopt dynamic pricing strategies during peak travel periods and promotional offers during off-peak periods.
Review OTA booking and cancellation policies and implement proactive communication strategies.
Maintain efficient room allocation practices while prioritizing guests' reserved room types whenever possible.
