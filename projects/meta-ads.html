05 / CASE STUDY

Meta Ad Performance Dashboard

Explore digital advertising performance to understand audience behavior, campaign effectiveness, and performance differences across Facebook and Instagram.

GitHub Repository ↗

Business Objective

The objective was to understand how advertising performance varied across different audience groups, campaigns, ad types, locations, and time periods. The analysis allows multiple performance metrics to be explored across these dimensions, making it easier to identify differences in campaign performance and understand how advertising activity varied across Facebook and Instagram. The report brings these views together to support a clearer evaluation of overall advertising performance.

Platform Analysis

Facebook Performance

Examines advertising performance across different audience, campaign, time, geographic, and ad-type dimensions, allowing multiple performance metrics to be explored interactively.

Instagram Performance

Provides the same analytical view for Instagram, enabling multiple performance metrics to be explored across audience, campaign, time, geographic, and ad-type dimensions.

Executive Summary

Brings key campaign, audience, ad-type, and conversion metrics together to provide a concise view of overall advertising performance and highlight the strongest-performing areas.

Dynamic Measure Selection

The dashboard uses dynamic measure selection, where the same visual areas can be explored using the selected advertising metric.

The selected advertising measure can be explored across age, gender, weekly trends, monthly trends, hourly trends, geographic views, and ad types.

Interactive Reporting

The report enables users to explore advertising performance from multiple perspectives through interactive filtering, cross-filtering, and custom tooltips. Dynamic metric selection allows the same visual analysis to be viewed using different performance measures, while the report structure connects platform, audience, campaign, geographic, and ad-type analysis in one reporting experience. Power Query, DAX, and data modeling support the reporting experience.

Key Insights

Engagement-to-purchase gap: The engagement rate was approximately 13.5%, while the purchase rate was below 1%, indicating a substantial drop between engagement and purchases.
Platform performance: Facebook generated higher overall volume than Instagram, while Instagram recorded slightly higher CTR and engagement rates.
Audience conversion: Engagement was more concentrated among younger age groups, but the 55–65 age group recorded the highest conversion rate.
Campaign performance: Campaign_12_Q3 recorded the highest CTR, while Campaign_27_Q3 achieved the highest conversion rate.

Recommendations

Investigate the conversion gap

Examine potential drop-off points between engagement, clicks, and completed purchases.

Test high-converting audiences

Test campaigns targeting the 55–65 age group and monitor conversion rate and purchase volume.

Optimize ad formats

Test additional video-based creatives and compare their CTR, engagement, and conversion performance with other formats.

Evaluate campaigns using multiple KPIs

Assess campaigns using CTR, conversion rate, and purchases rather than clicks alone.

Test peak activity hours

Since 8 AM was the peak activity hour, test advertising schedules around this period and compare engagement and conversion results with other time slots.
